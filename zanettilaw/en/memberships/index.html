Legal Netlink Alliance

Zanetti Attorneys at Law Ltd. is a member of Legal Netlink Alliance (LNA). Legal Netlink Alliance is a global network of carefully selected mid-sized forensic and advisory law firms, chosen for their high quality and integrity. Thanks to this network and the membership of high-quality law firms, the clients of the member firms receive the added value of high-quality legal representation worldwide when needed.
