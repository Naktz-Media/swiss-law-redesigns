Company Formation and Notarial Services – Professional and Straightforward Legal Support

As an experienced law firm, we offer competent and straightforward support in the formation of companies as well as in all types of notarial certifications. From the initial legal consultation to the final notarization, we guide you reliably through the entire process, ensuring that all legal requirements are met.

Whether you are forming a limited liability company (GmbH), stock corporation (AG), foundation, or another legal entity – we are your trusted partner, offering expert advice and years of experience. Our goal is to ensure your formation is efficient, legally compliant, and tailored to your specific needs.

Our Services briefly:

Corporate and Company Law

  • Formation of stock corporations (AG), limited liability companies (GmbH), cooperatives, and foundations

Notarization of corporate law matters, such as:

    • Amendments to articles of association (e.g. company name, registered office, purpose)
    • Capital increases
    • Transfer of shares (registered shares or capital contributions)
    • Legal transformations (e.g. GmbH to AG)
    • Asset transfers
    • Liquidations

Marriage and Inheritance Law

  • Drafting and notarization of marriage contracts
  • Inheritance agreements, optionally combined with marriage contracts (e.g. for spousal benefit arrangements)
  • Wills (last wills and testaments)
  • Advance care directives and living wills

Other Notarial Services

  • Guarantee declarations
  • Certifications (e.g. of signatures, copies, documents)
