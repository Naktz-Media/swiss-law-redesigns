Spheres of activity

Specialised lawyers give you advice in all fields or in certain areas of Labour Law, in particular in those relating to:

  • Private Labour Law (individual employment contracts)
  • Collective Labour Law (collective labour agreements)
  • Public Labour Law (Law applying to Staff of Public Bodies)
  • International Labour Law and Foreigners Law
  • Labour protection and Participation Law
  • Gender Equality Law
  • Data Protection Law, Unfair Competition Law and Intellectual Property Law
  • Insurances Law (occupational benefit plans, social and private insurances)
  • Placement Service Law
  • Procedure Law
  • Tax Law as well as Debt Collection and Bankruptcy Law
